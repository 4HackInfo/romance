FOR MY LOVE — ROMANTIC INTERACTIVE WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: all page sections and content
- style.css: responsive layout, animated gradient borders, effects and styling
- script.js: 30-image gallery, lightbox navigation, love rain, surprise messages and interactive buttons

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or another modern browser.
3. Internet is required for the remote gallery photos and Google Fonts. The page layout and text are local.

PERSONALIZE IT
- Change the hero and letter text in index.html to add her name or your own special memories.
- The 30 gallery image links and captions are in script.js.
- Change colors in the :root section at the top of style.css.
- You can upload your own couple photos later by placing them in an assets folder and changing the image src paths.

NOTE
The website uses generic romantic imagery, not real photos of your girlfriend. Replace the photos with personal pictures if you want the site to feel even more unique to your relationship.
